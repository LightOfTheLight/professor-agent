# professor-agent

AI paper reading skill for the Claude Discord bot.

**Features:**
- Daily brief: 3 recommended AI papers matched to your focus topics
- Guided reading: 4-prompt structured walkthrough (Abstract → Method → Results → My Take)
- Quiz gate: 5 questions generated from paper content; 3/5 to unlock next paper
- Learning stats: papers read, avg score, streak, concepts seen

**Commands:**
- `/professor` — start or continue a session
- `/professor brief` — manually trigger today's paper recommendations
- `/professor continue` — resume active paper guide
- `/professor skip` — skip current paper (re-surfaces after 14 days)
- `/professor stats` — show your learning stats
- `/professor topics` — update your focus topics

**Scheduled:** Daily brief at 9am UTC (weekdays) via Discord DM.
